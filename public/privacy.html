Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

JG Club Penguin ("the game") is a hackathon project: a multiplayer game set in a replica of the Jahnel Group office. This policy explains what the game collects when you sign in with Google and how that information is used.

What we collect

  • From your Google account: your name, email address, and a unique account ID. We request only the basic openid, email, and profile scopes. We do not access your contacts, files, calendar, or any other Google data.
  • Game progress you create: your Penguin's name and look, Tokens, Badges, Minigame scores, purchased items, and Igloo layout.
  • Chat and Presence: chat messages and your Penguin's position are sent live to other Players in the same Room. They are not stored after they are delivered.

How we use it

We use this information only to sign you in, show your Penguin to other Players, and save your progress between sessions. Other Players can see your Penguin's name, look, and chat messages. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers that run the game.

Service providers

  • Supabase: sign-in, database, and real-time messaging.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Google: sign-in.

Google user data

The game's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all saved progress, open an issue at github.com/ChelseaMerrill/JG-club-penguin/issues asking for deletion. You can also remove the game's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

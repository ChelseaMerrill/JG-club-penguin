02 · HOW A DOOR BEHAVES
IDLE
The door already drawn in the room. It gets an invisible click area the size of the door and its sign. Nothing changes visually.
HOVER → CLICK
The door glows cyan and a "click to enter" pill appears. On click the penguin waddles to the door, the screen fades to dark (250ms), and the next room opens with the penguin at that room's matching door.
LOCKED
For doors that lead to rooms we haven't built yet (Team Rooms 5–9, in Hallway B). The door is greyed out with a padlock. Clicking it shows a speech bubble: "Under construction."
KITCHEN ↘
The blinking exit pills at the bottom of each room become links too, so you can also leave from the HUD. Keyboard: Tab moves between doors, Enter goes through.
